GOOD QUESTIONS.

What’s AI SEO, and do I really need it?+

More customers now ask tools like ChatGPT and Google’s AI Overviews who to hire instead of scrolling through search results. AI SEO makes your services, service area and reviews easy for those tools to read and recommend. We’ve helped four Austin-area businesses start appearing organically in AI-generated answers.

How long does SEO take to work?+

Some fixes, like your Google Business Profile and site speed, can help quickly. Rankings usually build over weeks to months. Nobody can honestly guarantee a #1 spot, so instead we show you what changed each month.

What’s included in a website build?+

A fully scoped website with the pages and features agreed before work begins. You get mobile-first styling, your supplied copy, photos and branding, clear service sections and contact links, and two revision rounds. One month of extra support for maintenance is included, within the delivered scope. Turnaround is about 7 days once your content, access and deposit are in.

What costs extra?+

Pages or features outside the agreed scope, copywriting, booking or payment systems, custom integrations, ongoing local SEO, AI search optimization, AI tools, and maintenance beyond the included month. Domain, hosting and other third-party fees are separate. Everything is quoted up front.

Do you only work in Austin?+

We’re based in Austin and focus on the metro: Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Hutto and nearby. We work with businesses anywhere in Texas too.

What do you need from me to start?+

Your business details, copy, images you have permission to use, branding and any account access we need. We agree on the scope, quote, payment terms and schedule before work begins.
